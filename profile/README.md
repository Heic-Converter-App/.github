## 🚀 Overview
HEIC Converter for Windows is a lightweight HEIC file converter that turns Apple's HEIC photos into widely compatible formats. iPhones and iPads save pictures in HEIC, which many Windows apps, websites, and printers cannot open — this free HEIC to JPG converter fixes that in a couple of clicks. Use it as a HEIC to JPEG converter, a HEIC to PNG converter, or a HEIC to PDF converter, and process a single image or a whole folder at once. Everything runs locally on your PC, so you don't need an online HEIC to JPG converter and your photos never leave your device.

## ✨ Features
- **HEIC to JPG converter** — convert HEIC to JPG and JPEG in one click while preserving full image quality.
- **HEIC to PNG converter** — export lossless PNG files from your photos with transparency support.
- **HEIC to PDF converter** — combine several HEIC images into a single, shareable PDF document.
- **Free batch conversion** — drop in an entire folder and run this free HEIC to JPG converter across hundreds of files with no limits.
- **Offline & private** — a HEIC image converter that processes everything on your device, with nothing uploaded to a server.
- **Quality & EXIF control** — adjust output quality and choose to keep or strip metadata (date, location, camera info).
